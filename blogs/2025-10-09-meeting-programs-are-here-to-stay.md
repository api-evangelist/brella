The New Currency of Events: Why Meeting programs Matter More Than Ever According to Freeman’s 2025 Networking Trends Report, 51% of participants say that meaningful networking is the reason they return to an event. That means the real value isn’t in the keynote stage or the free coffee; it’s in the connections that spark across tables, hallways, and curated meetups.
